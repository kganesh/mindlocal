# Privacy policy

**InnerSage** is a private journal. It has no accounts, no servers of
ours, and no analytics. What you write is stored on your iPhone.

Last updated: 7 October 2026

## What is stored, and where

Your entries, the people you mention, your events, reminders and decisions are
stored in the app's own database on your device. They are included in your
device backups, iCloud or local, under your own Apple Account. We have no
access to them and no way to read them.

There is no sign-up, no profile, and no identifier that follows you.

## What leaves your device

Three things, and nothing else.

**Text sent to Apple for generation.** To turn an entry into structure, and to
answer a question about your own history, the app sends the relevant text to a
language model. Where the device supports it, that model runs on Apple's
Private Cloud Compute; otherwise it runs on the device itself. Private Cloud
Compute is built so that Apple cannot read what is sent and retains nothing
once the request is finished. We never see it either.

The app requires Apple Intelligence and does not run without it, so there is no
configuration in which the app works and sends nothing. Which of the two models
answers a given request is decided per request and is not a setting you
choose.

**Your location, for weather.** If you add a location to an entry or an event,
the app asks Apple's WeatherKit for the forecast there. Apple receives the
coordinates to answer that request. Your journal text is not sent with it.

**Model downloads.** If a downloadable speech model is ever enabled, the app
fetches the model file itself. Nothing you have written is sent in that
request.

## Health, calendar, microphone and location

Each of these is requested only when you use the feature that needs it, and you
can refuse any of them without losing the rest of the app.

**Health.** With permission the app reads sleep, steps and workouts so a day's
entry can be read alongside how you actually slept. Health data stays on the
device, is never sent anywhere, and is never used for advertising or shared
with anyone.

**Calendar.** With permission the app reads upcoming events into its own local
store so they appear on your timeline.

**Microphone and speech recognition.** Dictation is transcribed on the device
by iOS. Audio is not recorded to a file and not uploaded.

**Location.** Used to name the place on an entry and to fetch the forecast, as
described above.

## What we do not do

We do not collect, sell, rent or share your personal data. We do not track you
across apps or websites. There is no advertising, no third-party analytics, and
no crash reporting service. Nothing in this app profiles you.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its database from your device. Individual entries,
people and events can be deleted inside the app at any time. Because we hold
nothing, there is nothing for you to ask us to delete.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here and the date
